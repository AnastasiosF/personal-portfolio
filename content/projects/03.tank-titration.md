## The problem

Titration for liquid tanks is a short calculation that gets done many times a day, usually
standing next to the tank with a phone in one hand. A spreadsheet is the wrong tool for that
moment. The app asks for the handful of measurements involved, validates them as you type, and
shows the result immediately.

## Design decisions

- One screen, no navigation. Every input is visible at once.
- Large touch targets and numeric keyboards, since it is used with gloves or wet hands.
- Results update live rather than behind a "calculate" button.

## Under the hood

Plain Kotlin with Android views and `TextWatcher`s driving a small pure function:

```kotlin
fun titration(volume: Double, concentration: Double, target: Double): Double =
    if (target <= 0.0) 0.0 else volume * concentration / target
```
